Common Questions

Good to Know

If something is not covered here, ask when you reach out. There is no pressure, and your questions are welcome.

Request an appointment
What is medication for addiction treatment?

It is the use of FDA-approved medications, such as buprenorphine or naltrexone, prescribed and managed by a clinician to reduce cravings and withdrawal. It works best alongside counseling and support, which we offer too.

How soon can I be seen?

We offer same-week appointments at every location and by telehealth. When you reach out, we will find the soonest time that works for you.

Do you take insurance?

We accept most insurance plans, including many Medicaid and Medicare plans, and we offer self-pay. Our team can help you understand your benefits. Please ask us about your specific plan.

Can everything be done by telehealth?

Many visits, including assessments, follow-ups and medication management, can be done by secure video for people across the state. Some care may be offered in person, and we will let you know what fits your situation.

Will my care be confidential?

Yes. Your care is private and protected. We treat every person with respect and without judgment, at every visit.
