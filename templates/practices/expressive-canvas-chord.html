What A Session Looks Like

Every session is a little different, but most follow a gentle rhythm like this one.

  1. 1

    Arrive And Settle

    We start with a cup of tea and a short check-in. You can tell me as much or as little as you like about your week.

  2. 2

    Make Something

    Pick a material, or let me suggest a simple starting point. We work quietly or chat along the way. There is no right result.

  3. 3

    Talk If You Want To

    We look at what you made together. Sometimes it opens up a conversation, sometimes it simply helps. You decide.

Good questions

Things People Often Ask

If your question isn't here, ask it on our free call. There's no such thing as a silly one.

Do I need to be good at art or music?

Not at all. Many people I see haven't drawn since school. We focus on the process, not on making something that looks a certain way.

What if I don't feel like talking?

That's okay. Art and music give you another way to express things. Some sessions are mostly quiet, and that can still be useful.

Do you take insurance?

I'm an out-of-network provider. I can give you a monthly statement (a superbill) to send to your insurer for possible reimbursement. A few sliding-scale spots are open each month.

What happens to the things I make?

They're yours. You can take them home, or I can keep them safely in the studio until you're ready.

Can teens come on their own?

Teens aged 13 to 18 are welcome. A parent or guardian joins the first call, and we agree together how they'll stay involved.
