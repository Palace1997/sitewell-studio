A Gentle Check-In

Is Couples Therapy Right For Us?

There is no test to pass and no right moment to wait for. If a few of these feel familiar, a conversation with us might help.

  • You keep having the same argument, and neither of you feels heard.
  • Lately it feels more like being roommates than partners.
  • Something big has shifted, like a move, a new baby, a loss or a break in trust.
  • You care about each other, but some topics feel too hard to bring up.
  • You are planning a future together and want a strong start.

You do not need to be in crisis to come in. And if only one of you is ready, that is still a fine place to begin.

Good Questions

Before You Reach Out

Still wondering about something? Ask us on your free call. There is no pressure to book.

Ask us a question
Do we both need to come to the first session?

It helps, but it is not required. Some people start on their own and invite a partner later. We will talk it through on your free call.

Will the therapist take sides?

No. Our client is the relationship. We make sure each of you has room to speak and feels understood, even when you see things differently.

Can we meet online?

Yes. We offer secure video sessions for anyone in North Carolina. Partners can even join from two different places.

What if our teen does not want to come?

That is very common. We often start with parents, then invite your teen in a low-pressure way. Teens also get some time on their own with the therapist.

Do you take insurance?

We are out of network with most plans. We can give you a superbill to send to your insurer. Ask us about your plan on the free call.
