What We Offer

Support Built Around Your Child and Your Family

Every plan is designed and overseen by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst, and carried out with Registered Behavior Technicians your child gets to know.

In-Home ABA

One-to-one therapy in the rooms your child already knows, using the toys and routines they love. Skills learned at home tend to stick.

Parent Coaching & Training

We show you simple, practical strategies that work in real moments, so progress keeps going long after we leave for the day.

Early Intervention

Gentle, play-based support for younger children, focused on communication, connection and the everyday skills that open up their world.

Telehealth Parent Sessions

Secure video check-ins with your BCBA between visits, for coaching, questions and quick problem-solving when you need a hand.

School & Daycare Collaboration

With your okay, we partner with teachers and caregivers so your child has the same kind, consistent support everywhere they go.

Sibling & Family Support

Brothers, sisters and caregivers are part of the picture. We help the whole household understand, play and grow together.

About Hearth ABA

Neurodiversity-Affirming Care, In the Comfort of Home

We are a small group of BCBAs and RBTs who believe autistic kids deserve support that respects who they are. We never try to fix or cure. We follow your child's comfort and assent, and we build the skills that matter most to your family.

  • Every plan is written and overseen by a BCBA
  • Play-based and led by your child's comfort and assent
  • Goals you choose, built around real family life
  • Consistent RBTs, so your child sees familiar faces
