Services

Every child is different, so every plan is too. Choose what fits your family, and we can adjust as your child grows.

At the center

Early Learning Program

Play-based sessions in a calm, bright room, with a small team who gets to know your child well.

At home

In-Home ABA

Support in the place your child knows best, built around real routines like meals, bedtime and getting out the door.

Small groups

Telehealth Parent Sessions

Getting started

Getting Started In 4 Steps

Starting ABA can feel like a lot. We walk you through each step and explain things in plain language along the way.

  1. 1

    Reach Out

    Book a free family call or send us a short message. Tell us a little about your child and what you're hoping for.

  2. 2

    Intake and Insurance Check

    We gather the basics and check your coverage with you, so you know what to expect before anything begins.

  3. 3

    Assessment With a BCBA

    A Board Certified Behavior Analyst spends time with your child through play, and listens closely to you.

  4. 4

    A Plan Built With Your Family

    Together we choose goals that matter at home, at school and in the community. You can revisit them any time.

Our approach

Your Child's Comfort Comes First

We see autistic children as whole people with their own interests, strengths and ways of communicating. Our role is to help them build skills that make daily life easier and more joyful, while respecting who they are.

  • Play-based learning that follows your child's interests
  • We watch for your child's assent and pause when they need a break
  • Goals chosen with your family, not for it
  • Every plan overseen by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst

FAQ

Questions Families Often Ask

What does "assent" mean in your sessions?

Assent means paying close attention to whether your child is comfortable and willing to take part. If your child shows us they need a break or something different, we listen and adjust. Learning goes better when children feel safe.

How involved will we be as parents?

As involved as you'd like. Parent coaching is part of every plan, and you're always welcome to ask questions, watch sessions and help shape goals. You know your child best.

Do you work with my insurance?

We work with many insurance plans. We'll check your coverage with you during intake and explain what it means for your family before services begin.

Can my child get services at home?

Yes. In-home services are available from all three centers. We'll talk about what makes sense for your child and your schedule on your free family call.

Is there a waitlist?

Availability changes from center to center. When you reach out, we'll tell you honestly what openings look like right now and keep you updated.
