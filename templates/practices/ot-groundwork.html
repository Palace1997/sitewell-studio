What We Offer

Support for Sensory, Routines and Daily Life

Every plan is built around your goals and your week. Here is where our occupational therapists most often help.

Sensory Regulation

For bodies that feel too much or too little. We find what calms and what wakes up, then build it into your day.

Daily Living & Self-Care

Dressing, eating, hygiene and the small tasks that add up. We make them doable, step by step.

Routines & Executive Function

Planning, starting and finishing tasks. We set up routines and tools that take the pressure off your memory.

School-Based OT

Handwriting, focus, fidgeting and transitions. We work with students and teachers to help the school day go smoother.

Regulation Groups

Small, friendly groups where kids and teens practice calming and social skills together, with a therapist guiding.

Parent & Caregiver Coaching

You are with them every day. We coach you on what to try at home, so progress keeps going between sessions.

Dana Whitfield, OTR/L Lead Occupational Therapist
Our Approach

Grounded, Hands-On, Built for Real Life

Good Questions

Before You Reach Out

Not sure if OT is the right fit? Ask us on your first call. There is no pressure to book.

Ask us a question
Do I need a referral or a diagnosis?

Not to talk with us. Some insurance plans ask for a referral, so we will check that with you. You do not need a formal diagnosis to start.

Where do sessions happen?

In our clinic, in your home where many daily tasks actually happen, or online by secure video. We help you pick what makes sense for your goals.

Is OT only for children?

No. We work with children, teens and adults. Sensory needs, routines and executive function show up at every age, and the tools can help at every age too.

How are you different from talk therapy?

OT is hands-on and practical. We focus on doing the real tasks of daily life and building the skills and routines around them. We are glad to work alongside a talk therapist.

Do you take insurance?

We are in network with some plans and out of network with others. Ask us about your coverage, and we can provide a superbill to send to your insurer.
