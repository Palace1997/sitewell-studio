Good Questions

Before You Reach Out

Still wondering about something? Ask on your first call. There is no pressure to book.

Ask a question
How long does an evaluation take?

It depends on the questions we are answering. Most evaluations involve an intake, one or more testing sessions and a feedback meeting. We will map out the plan on your first call.

Do you take insurance?

Beacon is out of network with most plans. We can provide a detailed invoice, often called a superbill, to submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. Ask us about fees and insurance when we speak.

Will I get a diagnosis?

An evaluation can clarify whether a diagnosis fits, but it is not guaranteed. Dr. Shah focuses on understanding how you think and learn, and gives honest findings and recommendations either way.

Can teens be seen here?

Yes. Beacon sees older teens from age 12 and up, as well as adults. Parents are welcome to take part in the intake and feedback.

Can any of this be done online?

Intake and feedback can be done by secure video. Some testing is done in person for accuracy. We will tell you which parts need an office visit when we plan your evaluation.
