What a Coach Does

Steady, Practical Support for the Road Ahead

A recovery coach is not a therapist or a sponsor. This is hands-on, forward-looking help with the real parts of building a life in recovery.

One-to-One Recovery Coaching

Regular sessions built around your goals. We set small, doable steps and keep moving forward together, at your pace.

Family & Loved-Ones Coaching

For the people who love someone in recovery. Learn how to set boundaries, support without burning out, and take care of yourself too.

Telecoaching by Phone & Video

Meet from wherever you are. Secure video or a simple phone call, whichever feels easier on a given day.

Accountability Check-Ins

Short, regular touchpoints between sessions. A quick call or message to help you stay on track when it counts most.

Resource & Treatment Navigation

Confused by the options? I help you find treatment, meetings, housing and local supports, and make sense of the next step.

Small Group Coaching

A small, welcoming circle working on recovery together. Shared goals, real talk, and support that does not feel alone.

How Coaching Works

Simple to Start, Built Around You

No paperwork marathon and no pressure. Here is what the first few weeks usually look like.

1

A Free Intro Call

We talk for about twenty minutes, no cost and no commitment. You share what is going on, and we see if coaching feels like a fit.

2

We Map Your Throughline

Together we name what matters to you and set a few clear, reachable goals. Your plan, in your words, at your pace.

3

We Keep Going Together

Regular sessions and check-ins keep you moving. We adjust as life changes and celebrate every step forward.
