How We Can Help

Support That Meets You Where You Are

Everything here is free and led by trained peers who have walked this road themselves. Use one service or all of them, as often as you like.

01

One-to-One Recovery Coaching

Sit down with a recovery coach who gets it. Set your own goals, plan next steps and have someone in your corner.

02

All-Recovery Support Meetings

Peer-led meetings open to anyone in or seeking recovery, whatever your pathway. Listen or share, it is up to you.

03

Family & Friends Group

A weekly group for the people who love someone in recovery. Find support, ask questions and take care of yourself too.

04

Housing & Job Resources

Help finding recovery housing, filling out applications, building a resume and connecting with local employers.

Learn how to recognize an opioid overdose and use naloxone. Short hands-on sessions, and free kits to take home.

Who We Are

Recovery Happens in Community

Open Road is a nonprofit recovery community center. Everyone on our team has lived experience with recovery, so we know how much a friendly face can matter. Come as you are. There is no paperwork to walk in, and nobody will tell you which path is the right one.

Questions

Before You Visit

Not sure what to expect? These are the questions we hear most. You can always just call and ask.

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. Open Road is a nonprofit and every meeting, coaching session and training is free to attend. There is no insurance or paperwork needed.

What is a peer recovery specialist?

A Certified Peer Recovery Specialist (CPRS) is a trained professional with personal lived experience of recovery. They offer support, encouragement and practical help. They are not therapists or doctors, and we can help connect you with clinical care if you want it.

Do I need to be in recovery already?

No. Whether you are thinking about change, just starting out or many years in, you are welcome. We ask everyone to help keep the space safe and respectful for others.

Which recovery pathway do you follow?

All of them. Twelve-step, faith-based, medication-supported, mutual aid, or your own way. We respect whatever path works for you.

Can family members come on their own?

Absolutely. Our Family & Friends group meets every Wednesday, and family members can also book time with a peer specialist.
