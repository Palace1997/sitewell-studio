What We Offer

Ways We Walk Alongside You

Every service here is led by a licensed substance use counselor. Start wherever feels right, and we will help you find the next step.

Individual Counseling

One on one sessions at your pace, focused on what matters to you right now. No judgment, ever.

Group Therapy

Small, supportive groups where you can be honest and hear from people who truly understand.

Relapse Prevention

Practical plans and skills for the hard moments. Setbacks are part of the work, not the end of it.

Family Support

Space for the people who love you to understand, heal, and learn how to support your recovery.

DUI & Court-Ordered Services

State-approved assessments and classes, with the paperwork handled clearly and on time for you.

Referral to Higher Levels of Care

If you need more support, we help you find detox, inpatient or medical care, and stay with you through it.
