Belong Here, and Build a Life Here

Common Table is a clubhouse for adults living with serious mental illness. We are members, not patients, and we run this place side by side with staff. Come for the company, stay for the work, the routine and the people.

What We Do Together

The Work-Ordered Day, and Everything Around It

Choose what you take part in, at your own pace. There is a place at the table whether you want to work, learn, rest or just be around people.

The Work-Ordered Day

The heart of the clubhouse. Side by side with staff, members keep the place running, the kitchen, the office, the front desk and the newsletter. Real work that is needed, done together.

Employment Support

Transitional and supported jobs with local employers, with staff and peers helping you get ready, start out and stay steady. Begin with a few hours and grow from there.

Education Support

Finish a diploma, take a class or brush up on skills. We help you find the right course, sort out the paperwork and keep going once you have started.

Wellness and Daily Living

Shared meals, gentle movement, cooking and the small routines that hold a day together. A calm, steady rhythm you can lean on.

Social Events and Community

Evenings, weekends and holidays together, so no one spends them alone. Potlucks, outings, game nights and the friendships that come from showing up.

Help With Housing and Benefits

A hand with the forms that get in the way, housing, benefits, appointments and getting connected to the right services in the community.

About Common Table

Members Run This Place, Together

Common Table is a nonprofit clubhouse built on a simple idea: people living with mental illness get better when they are needed, useful and part of something. There are no patients here and no appointments to pass or fail. Walk in, find a job to do, and you belong.

  • Free to attend, for as long as it helps, with no referral needed
  • Guided by the international clubhouse model and its standards
  • Staffed by peers with lived experience and vocational specialists
  • Members help decide how the clubhouse is run, together with staff

A Typical Day

What a Day at the Table Looks Like

Come for an hour or the whole day. Nothing is required, and there is always a seat and a cup of coffee waiting.

8:30 am

Doors Open, Coffee On

Members and staff arrive, catch up over coffee and look at what the day needs.

9:30 am

Unit Meetings

Each unit, the kitchen, office and outreach, plans the work together and shares out the tasks.

12:30 pm

Lunch at the Common Table

A simple meal cooked by the kitchen unit, shared at one long table. Everyone is welcome.

2:00 pm

Work, Classes and One-on-Ones

Afternoon work, employment and education check-ins, and quiet time with staff when you need it.

4:30 pm

Wrap Up, and Often Supper

We tidy up together, plan tomorrow, and on many evenings stay on for a social event or a shared supper.
