The studio

We wait for the light.

Frame & Field began with a single second-hand camera and a stubborn belief that the best photographs are found, not staged. A decade on, that hasn't changed.

We work almost entirely in black and white, stripping away colour to leave only what matters: form, gesture and the quality of the light itself. Every commission is hand-edited in-house, shot on film and digital.
