Our approach

No shame, just support.

Change happens in a relationship built on trust, not judgment. Here is what the journey looks like.

  1. 1
    Confidential and non-judgmental

    You reach out

    Everything you share is confidential. We start where you are, at your pace, with no judgment about the past.

  2. 2
    Harm-reduction friendly

    We meet you where you are

    Whether you're curious about change or ready to commit, we build a plan around your goals, not a one-size template.

  3. 3
    Family sessions available

    Support widens

    Recovery rarely happens alone. Family sessions bring the people who care about you into the work when you're ready.

  4. 4
    Coordination with medical care

    You keep moving forward

    We coordinate with your medical care and plan for aftercare, so the support continues well past the sessions themselves.
