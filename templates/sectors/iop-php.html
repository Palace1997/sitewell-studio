A typical program day

Monday to Friday, home by evening

Evening and virtual IOP follow the same rhythm, in shorter blocks.

  1. 9:00am

    Check-in

    Settle in, share how the night went and set one aim for the day.

  2. 10:00am

    Group therapy

    A small, steady group led by a licensed clinician.

  3. 12:00pm

    Lunch together

    A proper break, shared with the people in your group.

  4. 1:00pm

    Skills session

    Practical tools for stress, mood and sleep you can use tonight.

  5. 2:30pm

    One-to-one time

    Time with your own therapist or psychiatrist, focused on you.

  6. 3:15pm

    Home for the evening

    Back to your own routines, people and bed.
