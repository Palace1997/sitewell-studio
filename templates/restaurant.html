Our story

Great ingredients, nothing to hide.

We opened Ember & Oak with one idea: cook honest food over live flame and let the produce do the talking. Seasonal vegetables picked fresh each morning, meat and fish from named farms and day-boats we trust, all finished over sustainable British oak and charcoal.

The wine follows the same instinct, low-intervention, poured by people who genuinely love it. The room is warm, the fire is loud, and the welcome is meant to feel like the best kind of dinner party.
