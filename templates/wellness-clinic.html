What we offer

Comprehensive care, one clinic.

From first assessment through to ongoing treatment, every service is delivered by qualified specialists working as one coordinated team.

01

Psychiatric assessment

A thorough, consultant-led evaluation to reach a clear diagnosis and a treatment plan you understand.

02

Medication management

Careful prescribing, monitoring and reviews from psychiatrists, with regular check-ins to keep treatment on track.

03

Talking therapy

Evidence-based CBT, psychotherapy and counselling matched to your needs, in person or online.

04

ADHD & autism assessment

Structured, gold-standard diagnostic assessments for adults and young people, with clear next steps.

05

Group programmes

Facilitated groups and structured programmes for anxiety, mood and recovery, supported by our clinicians.

06

Online consultations

Secure video appointments with the same consultants you'd see in clinic, care that fits your life.
